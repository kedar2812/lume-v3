## What's built

**Phase 7B: bulk at scale (API).**
- A bulk action covers up to 50,000 leads: picked ones, or everything a filter shows.
- Big runs go in batches of 500, with Stop. A crash resumes where it left off.
- Undo works for 24 hours, and only on leads nobody changed since.
- The audit log gets one entry per run, and each person gets one notice.
- Measured at 200,000 leads, for 49k leads: assign 11.7 s, tag 8.8 s, stage move 9.6 s (it was 199.5 s before I made it
  set-based), undo 13.4 s.

**Phase 7C: the screens (to the approved Phase 7 canvas).**
- **The floating island:** Move to, Assign, Tags, and more. A long run shows progress and can be stopped, or tucked into
  the top bar. When it finishes, it says what was skipped and why, and offers Undo.
- **"Select all N that match"**, and Recent bulk actions (the clock icon), with Undo while it lasts.
- **Search words:** the search field says how it's searching. A very broad search says it shows the newest 10,000.
- **Loading everywhere:**
  - the top progress bar;
  - rows dim, then turn into skeletons after 1.2 s;
  - "Taking longer than usual" after 3 s;
  - when offline, an automatic retry with a countdown.
- **Popups cover the whole window**, sidebar and top bar included, as you asked. A test now fails any popup that dims
  without the shared blur.
- **Profile photo:** initials on a colour, or a photo lined up in a cropper (drag with soft edges, zoom, turn, a thirds
  grid, live previews).

**Phase 8: Analytics.**
- **8A, the engine.** Every number has one definition, in the spec and in the code's metric catalogue. It is counted in
  the business's own timezone, at the viewer's reach, and opens exactly the leads behind it.
  - Daily rollups are refreshed every 10 minutes.
  - Wins are credited to whoever owned the lead when it was won.
  - **Proof:** a made-up business of 400 leads is checked against an independent calculation. Every number matches, for
    everyone and for each person, and every drill-down opens exactly as many leads as its number counts.
  - **At 1,000,000 leads:** every dashboard answers within 300 ms; the admin overview takes 70 ms, a rep's own 189 ms, timing 7 ms (30 days). The first
    run was 1.2 s; I found the cause and fixed it. Figures are in `docs/runbooks/performance.md`.
- **8B, LUME noticed.**
  - Fourteen detectors. Each speaks only with enough data, a real difference (a statistical test) and a difference worth
    saying, and in its own written sentence.
  - At most three show at once, and the same one isn't repeated for 14 days.
  - Goals show progress and the pace "at this pace", labelled as an estimate.
  - Sources can carry a monthly spend.
  - A Monday email of last week's numbers goes to those who see all analytics. It holds numbers only, no names. It can
    be switched off in My account.
- **8C, the screens (first build, to the approved Phase 8 canvas).**
  - Overview: a headline worked out from the numbers, 12 tiles with your trend rule, new leads by source, the funnel,
    Needs a look, LUME noticed, and goals.
  - Six more boards: Funnel, Team, Revenue & sources, Lost, Timing, and Templates & data.
  - Every number opens its leads.
  - Checked in Porcelain and Obsidian, with no accessibility violations.

## Decisions I made for you (with what each costs if wrong)

**7A, 7B**
- **Run lock (7B).** A queued run is locked while a batch runs, so a retry can't double a batch. Cost if wrong: none.
- **Undo protections.** Undo puts a lead back only if nobody changed it since. It won't return leads to someone who has
  left, or into an archived stage.
  - Cost if wrong: a person re-runs the action instead.
- **Assign undo for scoped roles.** Undo of an assign works for people who don't see everything. They may undo leads
  they handed on, judged on the owner they changed.
  - Cost if wrong: none.
- **Count drift (superseded).** I first ruled that a filter matching a few more leads by the time it ran would be
  reported, not refused. The 7C review showed a stale count on screen could hide a large gap. Now:
  - the island says "1,210 matched by then, not the 1,203 you saw" for small differences;
  - the API refuses a gap over 10% plus 25 leads (MATCH_CHANGED).
- **Capped searches.** A search past 10,000 matches can't be a bulk selection or an export. Cost if wrong: narrow the
  search first.

**7C**
- **Photo cropping.** The browser crops and encodes the photo; the API checks its bytes (exact 256×256 from the header,
  ≤ 200 KB, no metadata). This avoids installing an image library on every client's server.
  - Cost if wrong: a crafted file passing the checks is still served only as an image, with strict headers.
- **Photo coverage.** Photos show on the sidebar profile, lead owners and My account. Other avatars keep initials for
  now.
- **Onboarding and tour.** The onboarding welcome and the tour spotlight keep their own look.
- **Deferred to the polish pass.** The "rail" under the table rows, and the shake on a refused export.

**8A, 8B**
- **First contact and reply.** These live in their own table (`lead_firsts`), not on leads. Leads have strict row
  security that LUME's own background writers can't satisfy.
  - Cost if wrong: one join.
- **Edge-case definitions.**
  - A rescheduled meeting counts as booked on the day it was moved.
  - "On time" is out of follow-ups done.
  - "Replied within 72 h" doesn't stop at the next message.
  - Cost if wrong: small differences on those edges.
- **Forecast.** It reads the kept stage counts, so it follows the viewer's lead reach.
- **Not built yet.** Tag and custom-field filters on analytics; two of the 14 detectors (no-show slot and after-hours
  arrivals) aren't fed numbers yet.
- **Fixture answers.** They come from an independent reference calculation, not typed by hand. I didn't add a separate
  fixture for daylight-saving zones.
- **Weekly email default.** It is on unless switched off, including for people whose settings were saved before the
  switch existed.
