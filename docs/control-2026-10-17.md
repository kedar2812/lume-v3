## What the owner sees on license.lumecrm.in

- A client's page gains **Plan limits**, under Plan and price:
  - **Places:** No limit, or a number (a stepper, 1–10,000), with how many people use their LUME now and how many
    places that leaves free.
  - **Modules:** Google Sheets, Webhooks, Google Calendar and Calendly, each with its own switch. Switching off a
    module that's on at the client says so first: "On at <client> now — switching it off stops it at their next
    check-in."
  - **Save** answers "Saved. Their LUME keeps it from its next check-in, within 6 hours."
  - When the client's LUME is too old to keep a plan, the card says so and still saves; the plan waits for the
    update.
- The history records each change, for example "Plan: 5 places; Calendly not included".
- Fleet's People column shows "4 of 5" when a client has a limit.

## What a client's team sees (with the next LUME release)

- **Settings → People:**
  - "4 of 5 places" beside the Invite title.
  - When every place is taken, the Invite panel says so before anyone types, and Send invite is disabled: "Every
    place on this LUME's plan is taken (5 of 5). Pause someone, or ask LUME's team about more places."
  - Over the limit after a plan shrinks, it says "7 people use this LUME and its plan has 5 places. Nobody loses
    access; new people can be added once there's room."
  - Nobody is ever paused or removed. Restoring a paused person waits for a free place.
- **Settings → Integrations:** a module left out shows the pill "Not in this LUME's plan" and the line "Its
  settings are kept. Ask LUME's team to add it to your plan." Its switch is off and can't be changed, and Calendly's
  Connect is hidden.
- **When a plan takes away a module that's on:** LUME switches it off once, at that check-in. It writes an audit row
  ("integration.disabled", by the plan) and tells each admin once in the notification centre: "<Module> was switched
  off. It isn't in this LUME's plan any more. Its settings are kept." Putting the module back in the plan switches
  nothing on by itself.
- People who don't manage settings see nothing new.

## How it fits together

- **What the LUME says at check-in:** `accepts: ["announcements", "plan"]` and `health.modulesOn`, the module keys
  that are on now.
- **What the licence server signs:** `plan: { places, modules }`, only for a LUME that says it can keep one. An
  older LUME gets exactly the answer it always did.
- **Tamper-proofing:** the plan rides inside the Ed25519-signed answer, so a client can't raise their own limits.
- **No plan in the answer means no limits.** A licence server older than the LUME never sends a plan, and the
  LUME's plain-body retry keeps it checking in.
- **Counting places:** people who are active or paused, plus open invites. The count runs under an advisory lock in
  the invite's own transaction, so two invites at the last place can't both get it. The API refuses with 409
  `PLAN_PLACES` and the module switches with 409 `PLAN_MODULE`, so a stale screen can't get past either.
- **Storage:**
  - Licence server: `licences.places` and `licences.modules` (migration 0007).
  - LUME: `licence_state.plan_applied` (migration 0067) remembers what was already switched off, so the switch-off
    happens once.

## Rulings I made

- **Places count by the licence clock**, not the database's now(), so the tests and the app share one clock. Cost
  if wrong: an invite expiring within seconds is counted or not.
- **People works out places from the lists on screen** (active and paused people, plus unexpired invites), using
  the API's rule. The count moves the moment an invite is sent or revoked. Cost if wrong: a list cut short would
  undercount; the lists aren't paged.
- **LUME's Invite is an inline panel, not a sheet.** The count sits beside its title and the full notice under its
  lede. This maps the canvas's sheet onto the real screen. Cost if wrong: a layout tweak.
- **Open invites past the limit read "(5 of 5)", not "(6 of 5)"**, on screen and in the API. The over-limit line
  counts people only. Cost if wrong: copy.
- **Calendly left out shows only its heading** (the pill and the line): no Connect, no switches, no Disconnect.
  The connection is kept and its bookings are ignored. Cost if wrong: someone who wants to disconnect waits until
  it's back in the plan.
- **The Plan limits card is keyed by client, not by plan.** Keying by plan remounted the card on Save and wiped
  "Saved"; the e2e found it. Cost if wrong: a plan changed in another tab shows only after a reload.
- **The e2e leaves Calendly out** because it isn't connected in e2e, so no later spec finds a module switched off.
- **The official marks are copied unmodified** to `apps/licence/public/brand`, with a README pointing to the web's
  sources.
