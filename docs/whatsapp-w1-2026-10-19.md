# WhatsApp replies, part 1 (W1): what shipped (2026-10-19)

Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-18-whatsapp-replies-ai-design.md` §3–4 · Plan:
`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-18-whatsapp.md` · Canvas: https://claude.ai/artifact/Th846e32sumxBHbJC89FqW ·
Runbook: `docs/runbooks/whatsapp.md`.

The owner asked for this to be built without waiting for permission ("dont wait for the permission I'll do that side by
side"). It was built against a fake Meta while the owner sets up the Meta developer app; every decision is listed at
the end.

Ram's ask, in his words: sending 150 messages, then going back and forth between WhatsApp and LUME to see who replied
what. With this part, the replies come to LUME by themselves.

## What a client's team sees (with the next LUME release, once Meta is set up)

- **Settings → Integrations → WhatsApp** (admins):
  - one switch, and a plain line on what it does and what LUME keeps;
  - **Connect a WhatsApp number**, which first says what changes on the phone (broadcast lists, the Windows app,
    opening WhatsApp every 13 days), then Continue with Facebook;
  - each connected number, with **whose number** it is (a person, or the business), Disconnect (asked first), and
    "Connect again" with the reason in words when Meta stops letting LUME in.
- **On a lead, a WhatsApp tab**:
  - the conversation, oldest first by day: the lead's messages on one side, the ones sent from the business's phone
    on the other;
  - photos, voice notes (they play in place), videos and documents (a download with its name and size);
  - "Getting this photo from WhatsApp…" while a file is on its way, and words when it's over 25 MB.
- **A reply by itself does what "They replied" does:**
  - the stage's reply move;
  - "until they reply" repeats stop;
  - the lead's owner is told once ("Sneha replied on WhatsApp", at most once a lead every 10 minutes). There is a
    switch for it in My account → Notifications.
- **A message sent from the phone answers "Sent?"** for whoever opened WhatsApp from LUME in the 24 hours before.
  Nobody taps "Yes, sent" again.
- **Not a lead yet** (Leads → "Not a lead yet · 3"):
  - messages from numbers no lead has, newest first;
  - **Make it an enquiry** makes the lead with their name, number and messages;
  - **Not a lead** removes them, and that number never shows again;
  - they're cleared after 14 days, and LUME never makes them leads by itself.
- **Past chats,** when the person shares them at connect: kept only for numbers that match a lead.

## How it fits together

- **The relay** (connect.lumecrm.in) holds Meta's app secret and runs Embedded Signup. It subscribes each client's
  WhatsApp account with its own address at the relay (`/wa/in/<instance>`), so it needs no list of numbers and
  stores nothing. It checks Meta's signature and passes the body on, signed with that client's token. When the
  client's server can't take it, Meta hears 503 and tries again.
- **The client's LUME:**
  - `/whatsapp/in` keeps each message once: Meta's retries are one event. It's encrypted while it waits and cleared
    once processed.
  - A queue puts each message on the lead: the number owner's own lead first, then the most recently active, never
    two. A message is on a lead once, whether it came live, retried, or again in past chats.
  - Files are fetched with the client's own token to its own server (Postgres, 25 MB at most).
- **When the client can't take messages** (WhatsApp off, out of the plan, read-only licence), `/whatsapp/in` says
  200 and keeps nothing.
- **Security:** row-level security decides who sees Not a lead yet and files. The token never leaves the server
  and is never shown. Every new route is in the access matrix.

## Found and fixed along the way

- **A client's Caddy sent `/whatsapp/in` to the web app,** so every delivery would have been a 404 on every client's
  server. It goes to the API now, with a test that pins the route.
- **CI timeout:** the demo consistency test timed out on CI's slower runner; it now has the timeout its neighbours
  have.
- **Test setup:** row-level security hides leads from a connection with no person on it, so processing that needs
  every lead runs inside the job's own transaction.

## Rulings I made

The full list, with what each costs if wrong, is in the ledger (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-18-whatsapp/progress.md`).
The main ones:

- **The relay stays stateless:** each client's WhatsApp account is subscribed with its own callback.
- **One relay for everything:** WhatsApp uses Connect with Google's relay connection. `WHATSAPP_RELAY_URL/TOKEN`
  exist only so e2e can turn WhatsApp on alone.
- **Admins connect numbers** and choose whose each is.
- **Files are kept in Postgres** (bytea, 25 MB).
- **A live reply does what "They replied" does,** with one notice a lead every 10 minutes.
- **Not a lead** remembers the sender.
- **The canvas was built from without waiting for approval,** as the owner asked; the owner reviews it with the
  screens.
