# Team Chat 2B Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Threads, @mentions that notify, pins, search, voice notes, drafts on every device, messages that wait out a lost
connection, and photo thumbnails, on top of Team Chat 2A.

**Architecture:** The same as 2A: Postgres tables under row-level security, plain POSTs, one `pg_notify('lume_chat', …)`
per change. A thread is replies on a message (`thread_root`), read apart from the conversation. Mentions are kept as rows
at send, so notifying, "mentions me" and counts never parse words again. Search runs in an owner's function that applies
the same access rule itself (a text-search operator can't use its index under row-level security) and answers ids alone;
the words are then read as the person.

**Tech Stack:** as 2A. Voice: the browser's MediaRecorder, the existing file store and sniffer. Thumbnails: the existing
render service and `file_previews`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-team-chat-design.md`; canvas KTQD9PJUGQJy1U4ynACEro v6 (approved): the
thread pane, the mention menu, the voice note and "Also send to #sales" are drawn on Main, ChatDark and Phone.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in 2A's Global Constraints holds (copy, sounds, no outside fetches, both themes, 390 px, scale bar).
- A word, name or count from a conversation reaches only people who may read it: in a notice, a search result, a pin, a
  thread list, a draft or a thumbnail.
- A notice's title and body never carry a phone number or email, and a lead-thread notice goes only to people who may
  see the lead when it is sent; it opens nothing for someone who has lost it since.
- A voice note is at most 5 minutes; its bytes follow storage's limits and keep-for, like every chat file.
- Nothing is kept on a device after sign-out (the waiting messages of the person who left are cleared).

## Review Focus

1. Someone is mentioned in a conversation they can't read (a channel they're not in, a lead they can't see): they get
   nothing, and the sender is told once, under the message, who wasn't told (Task 2).
2. The connection drops mid-send, the tab is closed, and it is opened again: the message goes once, in order, or says it
   couldn't (Task 6, with the server already holding it).
3. A search by someone who sees few leads, where thousands of newer matches are in threads they can't see: their own
   matches are still found, and fast (Task 4, the scale test).
4. A thread's replies never count as unread in the conversation, never move it up the list, and a deleted root keeps its
   thread readable under "This message was deleted" (Task 1).
5. Forty direct messages in a minute: one line in the bell, never forty; reading the conversation anywhere clears it
   (Task 2).

## Edge-case sheet (per task, as named tests)

People: a mentioned person disabled between typing and sending; a team mention whose team was deleted; @channel by
someone not allowed; the lead moved away between a mention and opening its notice; a person taken out of a channel with a
thread open and a draft kept. Time: quiet hours east and west of UTC, a working day with none. Repetition: the same send
twice notifies once; a pin pressed twice; two people pin at once; a draft saved in two tabs (the later save stands).
Outside: the render service off (photos show as they do today); no microphone, permission refused, a recorder that
answers no data. Data: 4,000 characters of mentions; Hindi, Marathi, Urdu and emoji searched as typed; a one-character
search; a search of only punctuation; a 5-minute note at the size limit. Screens: 390 px, keyboard only (the mention menu
by arrows, Enter, Tab, Esc), a screen reader name on play, seek and record; reduced motion; a refresh mid-recording.

### Task 1: Threads

**Files:** Create `packages/db/migrations/0131_chat_threads.sql`, `apps/api/src/modules/chat/threads.ts`,
`threads.test.ts`, `apps/web/src/components/chat/Thread.tsx`; modify `messages.ts`, `conversations.ts`, `routes.ts`,
`live.ts`, schema, `useMessages.ts`, `Bubble.tsx`, `Conversation.tsx`, `ChatScreen.tsx`, `ConvList.tsx`.

**Interfaces:**
- `chat_messages.thread_root bigint NULL REFERENCES chat_messages (id) ON DELETE CASCADE` (a reply's root, never itself a
  reply: checked by a trigger that also holds root and reply in one conversation), `also_sent boolean NOT NULL DEFAULT
  false` (shown in the conversation too).
- `chat_threads(root_id pk, conversation_id, replies int, last_reply_id, last_at)`, kept by the send;
  `chat_thread_places(root_id, user_id, last_read_id, PK)`: whoever wrote the root, replied or is mentioned in it.
- Indexes: `chat_messages (conversation_id, id) WHERE thread_root IS NULL OR also_sent`, `(thread_root, id) WHERE
  thread_root IS NOT NULL`.
- `GET /chat/messages/:mid/thread?before` → `{ root: MessageView, replies: MessageView[], more, lastReadId, following }`;
  `POST /chat/messages/:mid/thread` (as send, plus `alsoSend`); `PUT /chat/messages/:mid/thread/read`;
  `PUT …/thread/following {on}`; `GET /chat/threads` → followed threads, newest first, 50, each with unread (≤ 100).
- `MessageView.thread: { replies, lastAt, faces: string[] (≤ 3 user ids) } | null`, `threadRoot: number | null`.
- Events: `e:"reply"` with `m` (reply) and `t` (root); a plain `message` too only when also sent.

**Decisions:** a reply without `alsoSend` never touches `chat_conversations.last_*`. Unread in a conversation counts main
messages alone. A "Threads" row tops the list when a followed thread has unread (a count on it), as the canvas's lists do.
The thread pane is a third pane from 1180 px, a full-screen sheet under 860 px, over the conversation between.

- [ ] Tests first: `"a reply joins the thread and leaves the conversation's unread and order alone"`; `"also sent is in
  both, once"`; `"a reply to a reply goes to its root"`; `"a deleted root keeps its thread, its words gone"`; `"a thread in
  a conversation left is gone"`; `"following: the root's writer, repliers; unfollow sticks until they reply again"`;
  `"the same reply sent twice is one"`; `"faces are the last three, each once"`; web: pane opens, replies land live,
  Esc closes, focus goes back.
- [ ] Build, mutate every guard, run `LUME_SCALE=1000000` chat scale (list and pages < 150 ms with threads present).

### Task 2: Mentions and notices

**Files:** migration 0132 (`chat_mentions(message_id, user_id, PK)`, index `(user_id, message_id DESC)`, read by whoever
reads the message), `packages/core/src/chat/mentions.ts` + test, `apps/api/src/modules/chat/mentions.ts` + test,
`notifications/notify.ts` (kinds `chat_mention`, `chat_direct`; alert switches `chatMentions`, `chatDirect` in
Settings › My account), `components/chat/MentionMenu.tsx`, notification centre rows.

**Interfaces:**
- Words carry `<@uuid>`, `<@team:uuid>`, `<@channel>`; `mentionsOf(body): { people, teams, channel }` and
  `partsOf(body): Part[]` (text | mention | bold | italic | code | link) in core, pure, shared by both sides. A token whose
  person is unknown shows as "@someone". Angle brackets typed by hand are words like any other.
- At send: people who may read the conversation (members; for a lead thread, checked as each of them, by the function the
  live stream uses) are written to `chat_mentions` and told after commit; the answer carries `notTold: string[]` (ids) for
  the line under the message. `<@channel>`: `chat.channels.manage`, or at most 50 members; else 400 in words.
- A direct message tells the others; a muted conversation tells nobody but by a mention of their own name. One unread
  notice per conversation and kind: a newer one updates it in place ("Maya Kapoor: 3 new messages").
- `PUT /chat/:id/read` settles that conversation's notices up to the mark.
- Quiet hours: outside the person's working hours the notice is kept and no pop-up shows; it is in the bell when they
  come back.

- [ ] Tests first as Review Focus 1 and 5, plus: `"edited to add a name tells them; edited to take it away doesn't
  un-tell"`; `"never oneself"`; `"a team's people, each once, whoever is also named"`; `"switched off in My account"`;
  `"a deleted message's notice says only that it was deleted"`.

### Task 3: Pins

Migration 0133 `chat_pins(message_id pk, conversation_id, pinned_by, pinned_at)`; anyone who may write in the conversation
pins and unpins; at most 50 per conversation; a deleted message's pin goes. `GET /chat/:id/pins`, `PUT|DELETE
/chat/messages/:mid/pin`; event `pinned`; a "Pinned" count in the header opens the list; each opens at its message.
Tests: twice is once; 51st refused in words; someone out of the channel; deleted; both at once.

### Task 4: Search

Migration 0134: `chat_messages.words tsvector GENERATED ALWAYS AS (to_tsvector('simple', coalesce(body, ''))) STORED`, GIN.
`lume_chat_search(q tsquery, conv uuid, who uuid, files boolean, me boolean, before bigint, max_rows int) RETURNS SETOF
bigint` SECURITY DEFINER, applying the access rule inline (a member's place for channels and direct messages; for leads
`lume_scope()/lume_user()/lume_team_members()` against the lead's owner and not deleted, as `lume_lead_search`), newest
first, at most 200, every parameter quoted by `format(%L)` or bound. The API reads the rows as the person, so the rule is
checked twice. `GET /chat/search?q&in&from&files&mentions&before`. Prefix match on the last word; words are joined by AND;
punctuation alone answers none. Opening a result: `GET /chat/:id/messages?around=<id>` (25 each side), the message lit
once. Ctrl K in Chat jumps to a conversation or searches words.
Scale: 1M messages, a person with 10 leads among 100k: < 300 ms.
Tests: Review Focus 3; a lead moved away drops out at once; deleted words never match; edited words match anew.

### Task 5: Voice notes

`chat_files.duration_ms int NULL`, `wave smallint[] NULL` (64 bars 0–100). Sniffer: WebM (EBML with an audio track alone)
and MP4 audio for chat uploads only (WhatsApp's own list is untouched). `POST /chat/files?voice=1&ms=&wave=` checks 1 s to
5 min, 64 bars; the size limit is audio's. The composer: hold or press the microphone, a live level and time, Cancel,
Send; a refresh or a closed tab drops it; permission refused says how to allow it. The bubble: play, seek on the bars,
1×/1.5×/2×, time; one plays at a time in the tab. Tests: limits both ends, a video named .webm refused, no microphone,
refused, zero-length, two notes playing.

### Task 6: Drafts and the waiting messages

`chat_drafts(user_id, conversation_id, body ≤ 4,000, updated_at, PK)` own rows alone; saved 1 s after typing stops and on
leaving, cleared by the send; "Draft" in the list. Files stay with the tab that put them up. The outbox: a send gives up
its wait at 20 s and tries again (same clientId) at 2, 5, 15, then every 30 s while the tab is open, at once when the
browser is online or the stream is back; a refusal (4xx) stops and says why; "Waiting for a connection" under it. Kept in
localStorage under the person's id (words and file ids, never bytes), read back on opening, cleared at sign-out and after
a day. Two tabs never send one message twice on screen (Web Locks around a conversation's queue).
Tests: Review Focus 2; another person signs in on the same browser and sees nothing; order kept behind a stuck one; a
message refused because they were taken out of the channel.

### Task 7: Thumbnails

`lume_chat_thumb(file uuid)` answers the stored hashes for a chat file the person may read; the existing preview worker
makes them from `whatsapp_files` by hash (it never sees a chat row), queued at upload; `GET /chat/files/:id/thumb` serves
it, or the photo itself while it isn't made or the render service is off. The bubble asks for the thumbnail at its drawn
size with width and height set (no jump as photos load).
Tests: off, pending, failed, a deleted message, someone who can't read it, storage's clean-up counts it.

### After each task

Edge-case tests first, mutations on every guard (ledger), types, lint; after Task 7: e2e (threads, a mention's notice
opening at the message, search, an offline send), the full gate, a fresh review, push.
