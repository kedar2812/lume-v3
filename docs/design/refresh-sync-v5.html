Refresh — out of the button, and back into it

The Refresh button itself lifts, grows into the pill and then the card (right beneath it, anchored to it), and when the sync is done the card shrinks straight back into the button, which says "✓ 4 new" for a moment before returning to "Refresh". The new rows glow in the table as it lands. Press Refresh in either theme, or play both.

Porcelain (light)
Obsidian (dark)
In the real app the numbers are the sync's own: rows read from the sheet ("Reading rows… 64 of 128"), new leads you can see, and rows merged into leads you already had. A rep sees "2 new for you"; nothing new → the card says "Up to date" and slips back into the button sooner.
  1. The button lifts and becomes a pill ("Syncing new enquiries…"), growing downward from where it sits.
  2. The pill opens into the card: sheet → drifting rows → LUME, the blue bar filling as rows are read.
  3. A green tick springs in; the count ticks up to the real number.
  4. The card shrinks back into the button as the new rows slide in and glow; the button reads "✓ 4 new", then "Refresh" again.
Reduce Motion: no drifting or morphing — a still card appears under the button with the bar and the count, then fades.
