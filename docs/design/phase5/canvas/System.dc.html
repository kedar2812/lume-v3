One meaning per colour

Colours

A colour always means the same thing, on every screen. No violet anywhere.

Blue — the one thing to do, and where you arePrimary buttons, the selected tab, today, links. Meetings on your calendar wear it too.
Sky — blue's light touchSoft fills, glows and the rule beside a meeting. Never on its own as a state.
Green — on, done, healthyA switch that's on, a meeting held, a connection that's working.
Amber — needs you nowStarting soon, an outcome to log, a connection to look at.
Red — failed, or can't be undoneNo-show, an error, Disconnect.
Grey — off, past, cancelledThings that are over or not running.

Switches

Green when on, grey when off. Everywhere, for every setting.

OnGreen: it's running.
OffGrey: it isn't.
Can't changeDimmed, with the reason underneath.
A switch is never blue. Blue is for actions, not states.
