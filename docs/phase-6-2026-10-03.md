# Phase 6: security against data theft — the overnight build (2026-10-02 → 03)

Built to the spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-phase-6-anti-exfiltration-design.md` and the approved canvas
(https://claude.ai/artifact/M8FauNrEgtF9T62PkF69ka). Three plans, each built task by task with tests first, one
fresh reviewer per phase and one fix pass. The runbook for admins is `docs/runbooks/security.md`.

## What's new

- **6A, the watch.** LUME counts contacts opened, different leads opened and send-queue runs. Past a limit it tells
  the admins (a live HUD, a notification, the daily email) and can pause the person: their sessions end, the act
  that crossed the line is refused, and they see "Your access is paused". Settings → Security has Overview
  (alerts, review: Restore / Keep paused / Dismiss), Rules, and Access limits (hours and networks per role). Lead
  screens carry a faint watermark (name · email · date) for people who can't see every contact.
- **6B, exports you can trace.** Export on the Leads list makes a CSV or Excel file of the current view, never more
  than the person may see. Every row carries a `LUME ref` code, and the file holds one made-up check row. Files are
  kept 24 hours, sealed, and every download is audited. Settings → Security → Exports traces a file found outside
  the business back to whose export it was.
- **6C, offboarding and activity.** People → Offboard: sign them out everywhere, hand on their leads (shared across
  a team by who has the fewest open leads, to one person, or unassigned), disconnect their Google Calendar, see
  their last 30 days, then one act whose steps tick in order. Security → Overview gains today's figures, contacts
  opened per person over 14 days, and who's signed in now (with Sign out).

## Rulings I made (each with what it costs if wrong)

### 6A, the watch

- The licence count is a report (no seat cap), so a paused person still counts as a member. Cost: one number one
  higher.
- Windows are measured on the database's clock, the same clock that stamps audit rows. Cost: none.
- Enable on a paused person is refused ("Restore it in Settings → Security"), so a pause is always reviewed. Cost: an
  admin without security rights asks one who has them.
- A sign-in refused as paused is audited as its own entry and clears the throttle (the password was right). Cost:
  none.
- Access limits list every role with "The owner is never limited" (the owner is a flag, not a role). Cost: none.
- The alert drawer's lead count is LUME's own count over every lead. Cost: a reviewer learns how many leads someone
  holds, which People already shows.
- The 5-minute sweep pauses with the follow-up clock when the licence locks. Cost: no watch while the instance is
  locked, when nobody can work anyway.
- The watermark is one CSS mask, right in both themes. A limit's stepper saves 0.7 s after the last tap (one audit
  entry). Security sits after Teams in Settings. Cost: none.
- Networks have no labels; the network you're on reads "Where you are now". Cost: a later label column.
- "Open {Name}'s audit log" opens that person's own acts. LUME's own entries are in the drawer's timeline. Cost:
  none.
- An alert that paused nobody offers Dismiss alone, with "LUME told you; nothing changed for them". Cost: none.
- The lead-screen watermark sits above the lead drawer and below dialogs. Cost: it faintly covers menus over lead
  screens.
- The near-limit notice shows once per hour per tab. Cost: an hour boundary off by the business's offset.
- Any "paused" refusal in the browser goes to the paused sign-in page once. Cost: none.
- The e2e makes its own person and lowers the limit to 5, then puts the rules back. Cost: none.
- No pixel baselines for Security screens yet (review copies only). Cost: a visual regression caught a phase later.
- From the review: "an upgrade to a pause tells nobody" and "watched admins see their own numbers" were raised to
  Important and fixed. The new defaults went into the changelog. Cost: none.
- Declined from the review:
  - Custom hours across midnight are still refused. Cost: a night-shift business can't limit hours yet.
  - The queue rule stays at 3 runs a day by default. Cost: one alert a day for heavy queue users.
  - Phone exposure through WhatsApp links is left to a later audit. Cost: a scraper using wa.me links isn't counted.

### 6B, exports

- The code alphabet leaves out L, 0, O, 1 and I. CSVs start with a byte-order mark so Excel reads accents right.
  Cost: one invisible character some tools show.
- The check row borrows its neighbour's stage, owner and dates, so it reads like any lead. Cost: none.
- A `LUME ref` column with codes that match nothing falls through to the check-row search, so a forged column can't
  hide the real mark. Cost: none.
- The file's name comes from the open saved view (else "Filtered leads" or "All leads"). It downloads as soon as
  it's made. Only its maker may download it. Cost: a browser may ask once to allow downloads.
- From the review:
  - The check row stays unmasked in a masked exporter's file: it's self-evident by design, and Trace matches it
    exactly. Cost: a masked exporter strips it a second faster; the code column still traces the file.
  - Custom phone and email fields are masked in exports. On screen they still show in full to masked roles, as
    since Phase 1, because masking there touches editing. Cost: masked roles read custom contact fields one lead
    at a time until a follow-up change.
  - Tag matching was rewritten for speed, guarded by a behaviour test (a timing test would be flaky). Cost: none.

### 6C, offboarding and activity

- No new migration: leads move inside the request's transaction, written into the assignment history as
  "owner_offboarded". Cost: none.
- The calendar step runs as the person (their calendar rows are theirs alone), then the admin's scope is restored.
  Cost: none.
- Sharing across a team: open leads first by open load, then closed leads evenly (this replaced my first rule
  after the review). Cost: none.
- People's Offboard button is neutral; the sheet's act is the red one. The old Disable dialog is gone (its API
  stays). The alert drawer offers Offboard as a third choice. Cost: one class.
- The calendar result says "N meetings went with their leads", not "upcoming", because the count includes past
  meetings. Cost: none.
- The Offboard sheet is 620px, as on the canvas. Cost: none.
- Activity:
  - A person's bar for today turns amber after a reveals alert today.
  - The figures open the Audit log at their action and day (a new day filter).
  - Signed in now is one row a person, yours first. Session ids never leave the server.
  - "Exports this week" counts from Monday. "Leads opened" compares with the median of the 14 days before.
  - Cost: a different week start (see the deferred minors).
- From the review, fixed:
  - An unlinked duplicate meeting no longer makes offboarding fail.
  - Closed leads no longer tip the open-lead share.
  - Only live sessions count (idle ones aren't "signed in").
  - "Failed sign-ins" names someone only when every failure is theirs.
  - Open follow-ups go with their leads, and a removed meeting's reminders are cancelled.
  - The preview refuses the owner and yourself.

## Deferred minors (small, not fixed tonight)

### 6A

- Parallel requests at the exact line may let a few extra acts through (bounded; the sweep catches it).
- The sweep scans a day for hour rules. One failed sweep transaction skips telling admins about alerts already
  saved in that run.
- The watch index isn't built concurrently (migrations run with the app stopped).
- A paused tab notices only on its next request.
- The burst chart's running total includes the lead-in for some rules.
- The near-limit notice always says "contacts".
- Rules: "last 60 minutes" shows for the daily queue rule.
- Access limits:
  - keyboard focus after a lockout;
  - no phone layout for role chips;
  - IPv6 ranges in "where you are now".
- The stepper has no arrow keys.
- The watermark uses the unprefixed mask and sits above popovers.

### 6B

- The example code in two strings uses an L.
- In a name-sorted export, the check row sits out of order and its Value is always empty.
- Tracing by phone alone fades as exports grow.
- Trace reads only the first worksheet.
- "Found by" can say the column was deleted when its codes were forged.
- The download filename uses the UTC date.
- A Download link to an expired file lands on a raw error page.
- A Trace result isn't announced to screen readers, and the download time has no date.
- A rare code clash gives a 500.
- Three tests are weaker than they should be.
- The Exports list stops at 300 rows silently.
- Excel files are built fully in memory and stored a third bigger than needed.

### 6C

- "This week" is Monday-based though Settings has a week start.
- The failed sign-ins figure counts lockouts too, but its link filters to failures only.
- Someone in no team defaults to sharing across the first team.
- "Open … audit log" shows to admins without audit access.
- The sheet's "today" uses the viewer's time zone.
- Narrow races:
  - a receiver's status isn't locked;
  - a lead reassigned mid-offboard can be overwritten;
  - a lead assigned mid-offboard stays with the person.
- A database error's original message can be masked in logs.
- Sign out is offered on the owner's row to non-owners (the API refuses).
- "More than usual" shows on a fresh install with no history.
- Unassigned leads' meetings stay with the offboarded person. Calendly meetings never move.
- Three small accessibility and URL details.
