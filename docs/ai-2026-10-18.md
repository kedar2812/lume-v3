## What a client's team sees (with the next LUME release)

LUME AI is **off** until three things are true: the server has a provider set up, the client's plan includes LUME
AI, and an admin switches it on.

- **Settings → Integrations → LUME AI** (admins):
  - One switch, a plain line on what is sent and where, and that it isn't used for training.
  - Two meters, the way Claude shows them: "This hour: 12 of 100" and "This week: 640 of 750 · resets Monday 12 am".
  - At a limit, a line saying which limit and when it's back.
  - Admins get one bell notice at 75% and one at 90% of the week, and one when either limit is reached.
- **In a lead, after a call or a note:**
  - LUME AI reads the call or note into a **suggested next step**: a line on what it means, then the stage, the
    follow-up and the tags it suggests, each ticked.
  - **Apply** does the ticked parts as the person. Each part is applied on its own. One that can't be applied any
    more (someone moved the lead, the time has passed, the role can't change tags) is skipped and the reason is
    given in words, and the rest still go.
  - **Dismiss** forgets the suggestion. Nothing changes without a tap.
  - A suggestion not yet used is still there the next time the lead opens.
  - Every note and logged call with words in it has a **Suggest next step** button, always visible (never
    hover-only).
- **Catch me up:**
  - Offered on a lead someone was handed in the last 7 days, or one that has been quiet for 14 days.
  - Read only when asked: three lines from the lead's own notes and calls.
- **At a limit,** the place where a suggestion would appear says "LUME AI has reached this hour's limit. It's back
  at 3:40 pm." It offers no button. Notes, calls and follow-ups work as always.
- **On a phone,** the buttons grow to a thumb's size and Apply takes the full row.
- **LUME AI's mark** is The Loop: one handwritten l that curls back on itself (`public/lume-ai-mark.svg`).
  - It sits on plain white in Porcelain and on the dashboard's black in Obsidian, with a soft shadow.
  - There is no gradient background behind it.

## What the owner sees on license.lumecrm.in

- A client's **Plan limits** card gains the LUME AI and WhatsApp module switches, and "LUME AI reads an hour / a
  week". Empty means no limit. The defaults are 100 / 750 for a team and 40 / 250 for someone working alone.
- **Dates are month first** everywhere, as LUME writes them: "Mar 12, 2027", "Oct 14", "Sep 28, 09:12".
  This was the owner's delegated ruling.

## How it fits together

- **One plug, any provider.** `AiProvider.read()` has adapters for Anthropic and Google, plus a fake one for tests.
  - The provider, model and key come only from the server's `.env`. No model is hard-coded.
  - Each answer must match a fixed shape (zod), or the person sees no suggestion.
  - LUME's cleaner then keeps only the business's own ids, a lost reason only with a lost stage, at most three
    tags, and a follow-up only after now.
- **What leaves the server:**
  - Only for a read someone asked for, with the switch on.
  - The facts sent: the lead's first name, its stage, the business's stage names, lost reasons and tags, custom
    field labels and values, and the last 20 history items (4,000 characters at most), plus the note being read.
  - Never a phone number, email, Instagram handle or web address.
- **Limits that hold exactly:**
  - Each read reserves a slot in its own short transaction, under one lock (`lume_ai_reserve`). The provider is
    then called outside any transaction, and the slot is finished afterwards.
  - So two reads at the last slot can't both run, and no read waits on another's provider call.
  - Failed and timed-out reads don't count.
  - The hour is a rolling 60 minutes. The week resets Monday 12 am on the business's clock.
- **Kept:**
  - One `ai_reads` row per read: task, model, tokens, time taken, and whether it was applied or dismissed. No note
    text.
  - The app may only mark the outcome and can never delete a row.
  - Applied parts write the normal activities, with `via: "ai_suggestion"`.
- **Plan:**
  - `ai` and `whatsapp` join the plan's modules, and the hourly and weekly limits join the signed plan.
  - They're signed only for a LUME that says it can keep them (`accepts: "plan2"`), so older installs get exactly
    the answer they always did.
- **Provisioning:** `provision.sh` takes `LUME_AI_PROVIDER/MODEL/KEY` from the client's inputs. It writes the
  server's `.env` whole, so a key added by hand would vanish on the next run.

## The model: not chosen yet

The eval is in the repository: `evals/ai/` has 50 call notes and 20 lead histories, all fictional, in English,
Hinglish, Telugu-English and Marathi-English. `node scripts/ai-eval.mjs` runs it.

A model ships only if it meets these marks:

- stage right at least 90% of the time;
- follow-up day right at least 90% of the time;
- never a follow-up in the past;
- never an id the business doesn't have;
- catch-up lines checked by hand.

**No paid run has been made.** It waits for the owner to approve the spend. The runner refuses to call a paid
model without `--yes`. Against the fake model the runner works end to end and, as expected, fails the marks.

## Rulings I made

Each one has a reason and what it costs if wrong. The full list is in the ledger
(`.superpowers/sdd/2026-10-18-ai-foundation/progress.md`).

- **Owner's delegated rulings (2026-10-18):**
  - Licence dashboard dates month first: done, 0.6.1.
  - Zapier and Make show their official, unmodified marks, from Zapier's own site icon and Make's developer-docs
    site icon (make.com blocks plain downloads). This ships with the next web release.
  - "Leave without saving?" sheet: next.
  - Integrations keep their full cards; no optional pronouns: nothing to build.
- **Limits:** held by reserve-then-finish rather than the plan's request-long lock. The lock would have made
  every read in a business wait on the one before.
  - Cost if wrong: two functions to maintain.
- **Applying a suggestion:** each part runs in a savepoint. A nested transaction rolled back the whole request.
  - Cost if wrong: none.
- **Applying a suggestion:** a stage moved since the read is skipped and said in words.
  - Cost if wrong: none.
- **What the API sends:** a suggestion carries `dueAt`, its follow-up as a moment, so each person reads it on
  their own clock.
  - Cost if wrong: one field.
- **Catch me up:** a lead's first owner isn't "handed" it.
  - Cost if wrong: a lead made and handed in one transaction isn't offered a catch-up.
- **Catch me up:** a lead never touched counts as quiet.
  - Cost if wrong: an offer on a brand-new lead, where the catch-up says only what's there.
- **Health check-in:** `ai`/`whatsapp` are reported only after the licence server has signed a plan with them.
  An older server would refuse the whole check-in.
  - Cost if wrong: the dashboard misses "LUME AI on" until it's updated.
- **Where the card differs from the canvas:**
  - LUME AI's mark in place of the canvas's sparkle.
  - LUME's own 34 px buttons, growing to 44 px on phones.
  - A Close button on Done, with focus moving to it.
  - A suggestion already waiting doesn't take focus from the lead's name.
  - Cost if wrong: CSS and copy.
- **Eval expectations:** they accept every reading a careful person would. A single strict answer would grade
  the note's ambiguity, not the model.
  - Cost if wrong: the marks are a little easier to pass.
- **Spend:** no prices are hard-coded in the eval runner, and no paid run is made without the owner's yes.
