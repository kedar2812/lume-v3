## What's in it

- **Design audit (Task 1).** Every approved canvas (Phases 5–8) and the earlier screens captured in both themes and
  on a phone (`LUME_REVIEW=1 --project design-review`), set beside its board, and fixed worst first. Found among
  others: the drawer's Source said "From a connected source" for every lead; on a phone nothing replaced the hidden
  sidebar (now the canvas's bottom tab bar); Today's "vs last month" was the same days of last month; the Settings nav
  was 22 flat items (now grouped); business hours now read "Monday to Saturday, 10 am – 7 pm".
- **Override audit (Task 2).** A test flags any screen class that overrides a shared Button property at equal
  specificity; three found and fixed.
- **Scale (Task 3).** At 1M leads: /today 484 → 8 ms, Today's tiles 27–31 ms, lists, search and the drawer 2–33 ms,
  every Analytics board 22–172 ms, live tag filter 1.5 s (budget 2.5 s). `docs/runbooks/performance.md`.
- **Robustness (Task 4).** A 30 s statement timeout on requests; a stopped query or no free connection is a 503 in
  LUME's words with Retry-After; lost database connections are logged, never a crash; backup and restore proved on
  the dev stack (`docs/runbooks/restore.md`).
- **Security (Task 5).** Every public table has row-level security or a written reason (`rls-coverage.test.ts`);
  `pnpm audit --prod` clean; headers and rate limits verified.
- **Polish (Task 6).** Tabs and option groups are one Tab stop with arrows, Home and End; every Settings page has a
  way back ("‹ All settings", or the section it sits in).
- **Accuracy audit (owner, Task 8).** `accuracy.test.ts`: every Overview tile's previous value and trend, the Team
  board person by person, a rep's own numbers, the timing heatmap, goals with pace, and Today's tiles against
  Analytics — all against answers computed independently from the fixture's script.

## Real bugs found (each fixed with a test that failed first)

- Overdue-now counted against the database clock in Overview, Team, My numbers, insights and the drill, while every
  other number used LUME's clock (50 vs the true 43 under a set clock).
- A cleared yes/no answer was grouped as its own value instead of "Not answered".
- Revenue's goal line was the business goal under a person or team filter.
- From the fresh review: "Export all data" could be cut off by an idle-transaction timeout (and that crashed the API);
  "took too long" never reached people (drizzle wraps the code); Today's day was the wrong day at UTC+12/+13; the
  month tile could show the owner's own or another team's goal over everyone's number; Needs you showed rows to
  people who couldn't act on them; done-today's list and count disagreed; the minute refresh skipped the day's work;
  a meeting with no lead was said as "‹title›'s call".

## Rulings (decisions taken on the owner's behalf, each with its cost if wrong)

- The drawer's Won/Lost wrap to a second row at 1366/1440 drawer width — cost if wrong: a More menu.
- No "Reading from" panel in e2e captures — the e2e server has no Google connection — cost if wrong: none.
- No RLS added to the 54 configuration, account and worker tables — each is reached only through permission-checked
  code or a join to leads — cost if wrong: a future lead-derived query without a join could over-read (the test names
  the three such tables).
- The Security overview's four numbers stay four cards (Today's language) — cost if wrong: one CSS change.
- No idle-in-transaction cap on the request pool — "Export all data" legitimately waits there — cost if wrong: a stuck
  request transaction holds a connection until the statement cap or the client goes.
- The connect card keeps "Only meetings with your leads." — it is what the default rules do and what lumecrm.in/privacy
  and the consent video say; title-word and calendar rules (an admin's later choice) can keep a meeting without a
  lead — cost if wrong: a promise stricter than a business's own rules. **Owner to choose wording.**
- Font sizes: every screen now follows the approved canvases' type and was compared with them in the design audit;
  no global type change — cost if wrong: a type-scale pass, if the owner still finds sizes off.
- Today: the send queue has its own card, not a Needs you row; Team has no "vs yesterday"; no percentage against
  nothing; the week starts on the business's first day; setup steps have no ticks; "Live" means the stream plus a
  minute refresh (spec, "Rulings made while building").
